# IdeaVault Pro — Venture & Idea Evaluation Platform

> A comprehensive 10-pillar market viability evaluation and decision platform for founders, solopreneurs, and venture builders. Hosted at [ideas.plotkai.in](https://ideas.plotkai.in).

## Features

- **Guided 4-Step Evaluation Wizard**: Step-by-step evaluation workflow across Thesis/ICP, Market & Demand, Feasibility & Cost, and Moat & Next Steps.
- **Plain-English Viability Guidance**: Jargon-free guidance and contextual scale pointers for every scoring dimension.
- **Dedicated Research & Evidence Logs**: Record competitor pricing, customer discovery quotes, and evidence alongside each score.
- **Draft Saving & Progress Tracking**: Save midway anytime with real-time completion progress tracking on idea cards.
- **Automated Heuristic Diagnostics**: Real-time alerts highlighting unit economics red flags, execution headwinds, or high-potential MVP opportunities.
- **Interactive Visual Radar Analytics**: Powered by Chart.js for instant multi-axis viability footprints.
- **Executive Evaluation Brief & Print-to-PDF**: Formatted one-click executive report for investor discussions or co-founder alignment.
- **Privacy-First & Zero Setup**: 100% client-side with LocalStorage persistence and JSON backup/restore.
